How a detail runs

Four stages,
start to handover.

01

Walkaround

We look over the paint with you, agree what is worth correcting, and quote before anything starts.

15 min
02

Wash & decontamination

Foam pre-soak, iron fallout remover, then clay across every panel to lift what a wash leaves behind.

60–90 min
03

Correct & protect

Machine polishing to level the clear coat, then coating or sealant applied panel by panel.

2–8 hrs
04

Inspection

A lamp-lit walkaround together. If a panel is not right, it goes back under the polisher before we leave.

20 min

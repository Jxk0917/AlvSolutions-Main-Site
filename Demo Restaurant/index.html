Our story

One cart.
Three generations.

In 2012 we sold birria out of a cart on the corner of Calle Bonita and Fifth — four hours a night, until the pot ran out. The recipes came from Elena’s grandmother in Jalisco, written on index cards that still hang above the pass.

We took the lease on the building in 2016. The cart is still parked out back.

If the salsa wasn’t ground this morning, it doesn’t leave the kitchen.
Elena Rivas — Owner

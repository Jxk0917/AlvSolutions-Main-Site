These terms describe how {{ site.name }} works with clients. They apply to website projects and care plans agreed with {{ site.name }}. For any project, the specifics are set out in the ALVSolutions Client Services Agreement before work starts; these terms are the general rules behind that agreement, not a substitute for it.

1. Domain ownership

You purchase and own your own domain, in your own name. {{ site.name }} may configure DNS and connect your domain to your completed site, but ownership of the domain always remains with you.

2. Website ownership

Ownership of the final, in-scope deliverables transfers to you once your project has been paid in full. Until then they remain {{ site.name }}'s. Ownership is subject to any third-party licenses or services used to build or run the site (for example, fonts, plugins, or platforms with their own terms).

3. Google Business Profile

Your Google Business Profile stays under your own Google account. {{ site.name }} may assist with setup, optimization and submission, but Google controls its own verification requirements and timelines, and no timeline can be promised on {{ site.name }}'s behalf.

4. Payment structure

Projects require a 50% deposit to reserve and start the project.

The remaining 50% balance is due once the website is approved and before it is published to your live domain. "Launch" means the point where the completed site has received your final approval, the remaining balance has been paid, and {{ site.name }} publishes the site to your live domain or an approved production host.

If you cancel before project-specific work begins, the deposit is refunded, less any approved third-party costs that cannot be refunded. Once project-specific work begins, the deposit is non-refundable.

5. Scope and extra work

If requested work falls outside the package or scope you agreed to, {{ site.name }} provides a quote before doing it. You approve that cost before any additional work begins. There are no surprise charges.

6. Care plan cancellation

Care plans run for a 3-month minimum, after which you can cancel any time with 30 days' notice. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current paid billing period, and payments already processed for that period are non-refundable.

At the end of the billing period, {{ site.name }} stops providing hosting, maintenance, monitoring and the included update service. Ownership of the website is as set out in section 2. After cancellation takes effect there is a 30-day handoff window, followed by a 30-day archive window. After the archive window ends, {{ site.name }} is no longer required to retain archived site files or backups, and recovery is not guaranteed. One basic handoff of the site to a compatible hosting environment is included. A complex migration may require a quote that you approve before the work begins. Any third-party hosting, migration, platform or service cost after cancellation is your responsibility.

7. Third-party services

Unless a service is specifically included in your ALVSolutions Client Services Agreement, third-party services and subscriptions are paid by you directly. Examples include domains, booking platforms, email services, premium plugins, premium fonts or media, ecommerce services, delivery platforms, payment processing fees, and other external software or services.

{{ site.name }} will not purchase or activate a paid third-party service on your behalf without your approval first. Third-party providers may change their own pricing, features, availability or terms at any time, independently of {{ site.name }}.

8. Late payment and hosting suspension

An overdue balance may affect hosting or other service availability. If hosting is suspended for nonpayment, the site is suspended, not deleted. Restoring it requires the full outstanding balance to be paid, plus a flat $75 reactivation fee before hosting is restored. This reactivation fee applies only to hosting suspended for late payment.

9. 30-day launch warranty

Every completed website includes a 30-day launch warranty starting on the date the site is published to your live domain. The warranty covers bugs, broken functionality, layout issues, and implementation errors caused by {{ site.name }}, limited to items within the originally approved project scope. Warranty fixes within that scope are completed at no additional charge.

The warranty does not cover:

  • New features or new content
  • Redesign requests or additional revisions
  • Changes you or someone else makes to the site
  • Third-party platform changes
  • Domain or DNS changes made outside {{ site.name }}
  • Unsupported integrations
  • Issues caused by external services outside {{ site.name }}'s control

10. Client content and assets

If you don't yet have a logo, photos, or other content, {{ site.name }} may recommend DIY options, use professional stock placeholders in the meantime, or offer sourcing and design help as a separate, listed service. Logo design or paid media is not included in a project unless it is specifically listed in your package or ALVSolutions Client Services Agreement.

Logo and brand files are prepared for digital use. They may be used as a starting point for print, signage, vinyl, decals or embroidery, but their suitability for physical production must be confirmed through the relevant vendor and their proofing process. {{ site.name }} does not supply validated Pantone, CMYK, vinyl, thread or embroidery specifications unless they are provided and validated in writing.

{{ site.name }} does not perform trademark searches or trademark clearance. You are responsible for confirming that your business name, logo and marks do not infringe existing rights.

Work outside {{ site.name }}'s in-house identity scope, such as detailed mascots or character illustration, hand-drawn illustration or specialized lettering, may require an outside specialist. {{ site.name }} may coordinate one for a listed fee. Fees charged by the specialist or their platform are your responsibility and are paid directly, unless explicitly included in writing.

11. Client inactivity

If a project is waiting on content, feedback or approval from you, {{ site.name }} sends a reminder after 14 days without it.

After 30 days without a response, {{ site.name }} sends a pause notice and the project is paused. After 60 days, and after notice to you, the project is administratively closed. Reopening a closed project is at {{ site.name }}'s discretion, and there is no automatic reactivation fee. The $75 reactivation fee in section 8 applies only to hosting suspended for late payment.

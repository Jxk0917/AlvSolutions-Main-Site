Questions about the price.

What does the monthly maintenance actually cover?
Hosting, the SSL certificate, and keeping the site running and current. On Growth it also covers three updates a month; on Pro, unlimited. It is not a retainer for work you never asked for.
Do I have to pay the whole amount up front?
No. We agree the total before I start, and I take a deposit to begin. The balance is due at launch, not before.
What happens if I want changes after launch?
Small changes are website updates, priced by size — and included in the monthly allowance on Growth and Pro. Changes during the build are revision rounds, and each package includes some. Beyond those, extra rounds are a listed fee.
Can I start on Starter and move up later?
Yes. Adding a menu page, a catering page or booking to an existing site is exactly what the add-ons are for, and you pay the à-la-carte price rather than the difference between packages.
Do I own the site and the domain?
Yes. The domain is registered in your name and the site is yours. The monthly plan covers hosting and updates, not custody of your own business.
